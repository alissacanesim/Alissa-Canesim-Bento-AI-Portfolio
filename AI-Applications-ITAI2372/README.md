# AI Applications and Case Histories — ITAI 2372

This folder contains assignments and case studies completed for the **AI Applications and Case Histories** course at Houston City College.

## Course Overview

This course explores real-world applications of artificial intelligence across multiple industries through research, case studies, and critical analysis. Topics include AI adoption in government, healthcare, manufacturing, financial services, ethics, governance, and the future impact of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

## Activities and Projects

The coursework in this folder includes research assignments and case studies involving:

- AI applications across multiple industries
- Government AI and fraud detection
- Healthcare AI and clinical decision support
- Manufacturing AI and predictive maintenance
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
- AI governance and responsible AI
- Explainable AI (XAI)
- Ethical considerations and AI risk management
- Real-world AI implementation analysis
- Research-based AI innovation proposals

Additional assignments and supporting materials are included as part of the course portfolio.
