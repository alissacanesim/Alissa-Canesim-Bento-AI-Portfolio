# L01 - Exploring Real-World Applications of Computer Vision

## Overview

For this lab, I explored **medical image analysis** as a real-world application of Computer Vision. The paper explains how computer vision systems assist healthcare professionals by interpreting medical images, identifying abnormalities, and supporting clinical decision-making.

## Objectives

- Explore a real-world application of Computer Vision.
- Explain how computer vision technologies process medical images.
- Discuss the benefits and limitations of AI-assisted medical image analysis.
- Reflect on the future impact of this technology on society.

## Topics Covered

- Medical image analysis
- Image classification
- Object detection
- Image segmentation
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- U-Net architecture
- Explainable AI
- Responsible AI and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

## Technologies and Concepts

- Computer Vision
- Artificial Intelligence
- Deep Learning
- Medical Imaging
- CNNs
- U-Net
- Explainable AI (XAI)
- NIST AI RMF

## Result

The analysis found that medical image analysis can improve efficiency, consistency, and diagnostic support through classification, detection, and segmentation, but its safe use depends on representative data, privacy protection, explainability, and continued human oversight.
